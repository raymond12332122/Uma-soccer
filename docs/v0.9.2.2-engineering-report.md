# v0.9.2.2 — Reproduce the actual rendered bugs

Both failures in the recording are reproduced, root-caused and fixed. Neither
cause was what the previous pass assumed, and in one case the previous pass
had introduced the misclassification itself.

The recording was the benchmark: 19.3 s at 60 fps, decoded to frames and read
directly.

---

## 1. The giant screen artifact — it is the STADIUM STANDS, unlit

### Reproduced

Frame 26 (~13 s) and frame 29 (~14.5 s) of the recording show a large
white/grey angular slab entering from the right with hard black wedges
radiating across the pitch. The same thing is present in my own v0.9.2
playtest screenshots near a goal — which I had looked at and dismissed at the
time as "the void beyond the pitch edge". That was wrong, and it meant I spent
v0.9.2.1 hunting the wrong thing.

### Isolated

`tests/diag_artifact_isolate.gd` freezes the match, points the camera at a
goal, and re-renders the identical view thirteen times changing exactly one
thing each time. Fraction of frame below luminance 0.20:

```
00_baseline              0.1454
01_light_shadow_off      0.1337     <- shadows are NOT it
03_goals_hidden          0.1472     <- goals are not it
05_stands_hidden         0.0263     <- 82% of the dark pixels
07_walls_hidden          0.1464     <- walls are not it
09_players_hidden        0.1422     <- characters are not it
11_player_shadows_off    0.1434     <- character shadows are not it
```

**It is the stands, and it is their geometry rather than their shadow** —
turning the directional light's shadows off barely moved the number, while
hiding the stands removed almost all of it.

### Root cause

`scenes/Main.tscn` had:

```
ambient_light_source = 2          # AMBIENT_SOURCE_COLOR
ambient_light_sky_contribution = 1.0
```

In Godot 4 `AmbientSource` is `BG=0, DISABLED=1, COLOR=2, SKY=3`. So ambient
was set to **COLOR mode with `ambient_light_color` never set — i.e. black** —
and the `sky_contribution = 1.0` sitting next to it does nothing, because it
only applies in SKY mode. A `ProceduralSkyMaterial` and `Sky` were defined and
went unused for lighting.

Every surface facing away from the single directional light therefore received
**zero** ambient and was crushed to black. The stands are the largest such
surfaces in the scene, so as the camera follows play they sweep into view as
enormous black slabs — and the lit faces of the near stand read as the
white/grey slab. That is exactly "real mesh geometry being transformed into
view".

**Fix:** one line, `ambient_light_source = 3` (SKY), which is plainly what the
scene was written to do.

### Verified in rendered output

Same isolation sweep after the fix: baseline dark **0.1454 → 0.0952**. The
stands now render as blue-grey stadium structures rather than a void, and the
whole scene is correctly lit. Confirmed visually against the same camera.

**This is not a fix in the animation layer, and nothing in the animation or
retarget path was involved.** Root translation stripping, the BoneMap layer,
accessory bones and mesh deformation were all checked and all clear — see
section 4.

## 2. Grounded/keeper-looking poses — runtime traced, and NOT a role leak

### Runtime proof

`tests/diag_anim_trace.gd` records every animation selection during a live
match: who, their role, what was requested, what it resolved to, its category
and the outcome (brief section 5's debug mode, `AnimationController.debug_trace`,
off by default and never shipped enabled).

Over 45 s before the fix:

```
outfield players that PLAYED a goalkeeper clip: 0
refusals (wrong role, correctly blocked):       0
```

**The role gate holds at runtime.** Strikers, midfielders and defenders never
selected a goalkeeper clip. The v0.9.2.1 role separation was correct and was
not the problem.

What the trace did show:

```
TACKLE            39 of 181 selections (21.5%)
  challenge_slide 23
  challenge       16
outfield player-time inside a grounded clip: 3.8%
worst single player: 20.0% of the match on the floor
outfield players that spent ANY time grounded: 12 of 20
```

### Root cause — a misclassification I introduced in v0.9.2

`tests/diag_clip_posture.gd` classifies every mapped clip by **real motion**,
sampling hips height against the same rig's standing rest height across the
window the game actually plays (brief section 3). All three tackle clips:

```
soccer tackle       min hips 0.16 of rest   GROUNDED
soccer tackle (3)   min hips 0.18 of rest   GROUNDED
soccer tackle (2)   min hips 0.23 of rest   GROUNDED
```

v0.9.2 called `soccer tackle (3)` the **standing** challenge, on the grounds
that it has zero root travel while the other two cover 4 m. Zero travel does
not mean upright: it is a tackle from a standstill that still puts the player
on the floor. That is precisely the "classified from filenames and heuristics"
error section 3 warned about, and it was mine.

Compounding it, `_on_challenge_animate` played a tackle clip on **every**
`challenge_started` — which `BallContest` emits whenever any defender begins
accumulating pressure, i.e. constantly. So ordinary pressing dropped outfield
players to the ground several times a minute each.

### Fix

- There is now **one** challenge intent, `challenge_slide`, holding all three
  grounded clips, and it fires **only** on a committed slide (`kind == "slide"`,
  which `begin_slide` emits). Ordinary pressure plays no full-body clip —
  the pack contains no upright standing-tackle clip, and locomotion already
  shows a defender closing in. Same reasoning already applied to dribble
  knock-ons.
- Winning the ball no longer routes through the `"tackle"` alias (which now
  resolves to those grounded clips); it plays the procedural reaction.
- `scissor kick` is **deferred**: measured at hips 0.14 of rest, it is a
  genuine bicycle kick, correct for a spectacular overhead volley and wrong
  for the ordinary airborne-ball shot that was triggering it — the same
  complaint again. Deferred rather than reassigned blindly, per section 3.

### Measured after

```
                                   before    after
grounded outfield player-time        3.8%     0.3%
worst single player                 20.0%     3.4%
players ever grounded              12 of 20  4 of 20
TACKLE share of selections          21.5%     7.9%
outfield players playing a GK clip      0        0
refusals                                0        0
```

The remaining 0.3% is real committed slides and one trip, which is what those
clips are for.

## 3. Clip classification from real motion

`diag_clip_posture` now classifies all 38 mapped clip instances as
UPRIGHT / CROUCH / GROUNDED from measured hips height. Twelve are GROUNDED:
the three tackles, the two keeper dives, three body blocks, the trip, fallen
idle, standing up, and the scissor kick. Every one is either correct for its
intent (dives, blocks, falls) or has been deferred.

This measurement is now the classification of record; filenames are not
trusted for posture.

---

## 4. Root translation and retarget validation — checked, all clear

The artifact "strongly suggests a transform/deformation issue", so this was
checked before concluding otherwise:

- **Bone envelope:** `diag_artifact_hunt` watched every bone of every animated
  player for 2400 frames against each rig's rest extent. No bone ever left its
  envelope; hips vertical peaked at 0.63× rest.
- **Root translation:** asserted across the whole shipped library — no clip
  translates across the ground (worst residual 0.000000), including every
  newly activated clip.
- **Retarget layer:** unchanged and intact. The generated BoneMaps are
  preserved; 22/22 humanoid slots on all 11 rigs, 54/54 clips compatible.
- **Render bounds:** the v0.9.2.1 `custom_aabb` correction stays (192 m →
  3.58 m). It was a genuine defect and remains fixed; the isolation sweep now
  shows it was not the artifact in the recording.

Nothing in the animation or retarget path was implicated. The cause was a
lighting flag.

## 5. Debug overlay

`AnimationController.debug_trace` (static, default **false**) records every
selection with role, request, resolution, category and outcome.
`clear_trace()` resets it and `TRACE_LIMIT` caps memory. It is enabled only by
diagnostics and is off in the shipped APK — there is no debug UI in the build.

## 6. Rendered validation

Full rendered match from the real gameplay camera, plus the frozen-camera
isolation sweep:

- No giant black mass; the stands read as stadium structure.
- 22/22 players animated, rendered height 1.600 m.
- Players upright and running; grounded poses down to 0.3% of player-time.
- Locomotion mix 50% forward / 46% idle / 4% lateral, playback rate median
  0.55, 4% at the ceiling.
- Match plays through normally.

## Honest notes

**The stands are still dark on their shaded faces.** Their albedo is
0.18–0.32 blue-grey by design. They now read as a stadium rather than as a
void, but if you want them brighter that is a material choice, not a bug.

**One earlier intermittent.** A run of the QA suite showed 2 cross-role
refusals that I could not reproduce in three subsequent runs (0 in a 75 s
trace, 0 in two full suite runs). The assertion now names the offending player
and intent if it recurs, rather than only counting.

**Unchanged from v0.9.2:** sprinting still slides at the playback ceiling
(4% of samples), and mobile performance remains an extrapolation.
