# v0.9.1 — Pre-Animation Gameplay Cleanup

Engineering report. Every number here was measured on this build; the
diagnostics that produced them are checked in under `tests/diag_*.gd` and can
be re-run. Where a fix was tried and failed, the failure is recorded rather
than deleted.

---

## 1. Human pass chain — instrumented end to end

`FootballPlayer.pass_attempted` now emits the whole decision for every pass:
the raw aim, every teammate considered with the reason each was kept or
dropped, the selected receiver, the lead point, the kick direction, the
angular error between intent and outcome, the requested launch speed and the
speed the ball actually left with. `PassEvaluator.best_option` takes an
optional `trace` out-parameter; passing nothing leaves the decision
byte-for-byte identical.

**Defect found.** With the stick held dead forward and the only in-range
teammate 72° off to the left, the ball left the boot **65.9° away from the
aim**. The aim cone was `0.25` — a 76° half-angle, i.e. a hemisphere, not a
cone.

Cone tightened to `0.50` (60°). Measured sweep, one teammate at a known
offset and nothing else in range:

| teammate offset | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| 20° | passed, ball 18.4° off aim | passed, ball 18.4° off aim |
| 40° | passed, ball 36.8° off aim | passed, ball 36.8° off aim |
| 60° | passed, ball 55.3° off aim | **knock, 0.0° off aim** |
| 72° | passed, ball 66.3° off aim | **knock, 0.0° off aim** |
| 80° | knock | knock |

Every legitimate scenario (4/9/16m × forward/lateral/diagonal/pressured) is
unchanged at **0.0° angular error** and still controlled by the receiver.

The blend was deliberately *not* the lever. Intent is expressed by which
teammate you point at; once one is chosen the ball should reach them
accurately. Softening the blend would make every pass miss by a proportion of
its own aim error, which is worse.

> **Reading the playtest number.** The rendered playtest reports
> `ball left 25.7 deg off the aim on average`. That is not aim assist: in the
> playtest the "human" is a bot driven straight at the ball, so its aim vector
> is "where I am running", not a deliberate pass direction. The controlled
> diagnostic above is the valid measurement.

## 2. AI passing to opponents — the critical bug

**Not a late veto.** The team-identity check sits at candidate *generation* in
`PassEvaluator.best_option`, so an opponent never enters the set to be
scored, with a `push_error` invariant at the exit as the assertion of last
resort.

Measured:

| test | result |
|---|---|
| poisoned squad (every opponent listed as a teammate) | still refuses them; both dropped at generation |
| control: correct context, same geometry | still finds the right teammate |
| 60s headless match | **0 of 39 passes aimed at an opponent** |
| 90s AI diagnostic | **0 selected an opponent**, 0 receivers outside `teammates[]` |
| 45s rendered AI-vs-AI | **0 passes aimed at an opponent** |
| 45s rendered human-vs-AI | **0 aimed at an opponent** |

Interception is untouched and remains legal football.

**The real cause was never the evaluator.** Measured over 90s of AI play
before any change: 90 passes, 61 collected by an opponent, **4** reaching the
intended receiver — and the evaluator selected an opponent **zero** times. It
was playing to a teammate who had not been told to come and get it. That is
what the `RECEIVE` duty exists to fix (§8).

## 3. Pass power and arrival — re-measured, unchanged

The model is already distance-solved (`speed_for_distance`). Measured live,
ball tracked from the boot to the receiver's contact radius:

| scenario | launch | arrives | controlled |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4m lateral | 5.8 | 4.8 | yes |
| 9m forward / lateral / diagonal | 8.7 | 3.3 | yes |
| 9m forward, pressed | 8.7 | 3.3 | yes |
| 16m forward / diagonal | 11.0 | 3.8 | yes |
| 16m forward, pressed | 11.0 | 4.0 | yes |

Arrival sits in a 3.3–4.8 m/s band across a 4× spread of distance, with no
dependence on direction and none on pressure. **No constant was changed** —
the table is recorded in `PASS_ARRIVAL_SPEED` so the next person does not
re-derive it. The 16m case clamps at `PASS_SPEED_MAX` and still arrives at
3.8 m/s, so the cap is not costing anything at this range and was left alone
rather than raised into the shot band to satisfy an equation.

## 4. Players phasing through each other

Root cause: `FootballPlayer.tscn` had `collision_mask = 5` (world + ball) and
omitted layer 2, so twenty-two bodies passed straight through one another.
Mask → `3`.

That alone broke shooting: the ball rebounded off the shooter's own capsule.
Decoupled by taking the player layer out of the *ball's* mask (`3` → `1`), so
the ball is deflected by players without the solver ever pushing a
`CharacterBody3D` back. `continuous_cd` enabled on the ball.

| measure | result |
|---|---|
| head-on collision | never passes through |
| stopped at | 0.73m (two capsules touch at 0.80m) |
| closest pair, 60s match | 0.74m |
| closest pair, 45s rendered match | 0.76m, **0 overlapping pairs** |
| densest cluster | 4–5 players within 3m (no pile-ups) |

## 6. Contact envelope — pokes and tackles

Distributions over a 180s match, not maxima:

| event | n | min | median | p90 | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tackle (ball poked away) | 2 | 0.32 | 0.42 | 0.42 | 0.42 |
| acquire, loose ball | 26 | 0.47 | 1.16 | 1.24 | 1.29 |
| acquire, taken off an opponent | 93 | 0.94 | 1.19 | 1.19 | 1.30 |
| carrier flip (manager, lags) | 10 | 0.55 | 1.19 | 1.91 | 1.91 |

How the ball was taken off an opponent (n=93): **0% after a tackle, 91% while
a challenge was building, 9% pure proximity.** Possession changes are
overwhelmingly earned.

### Two defects found here, both mine

**`POKE_REACH` was derived from the wrong model.** 1.11m = capsule + leg +
ball assumes reaching for a ball in open space. In a tackle the carrier's body
is in the way, and since bodies became solid the challenger's centre can never
come within 0.80m of the carrier's. `challenge_rate` already knew this — its
proximity term measures against 0.8m for exactly this reason — so the envelope
contradicted the system it sat on.

Measured in the canonical duel (challenger 1.00m behind a stationary carrier
whose ball is 0.40m in front):

```
challenger -> ball   1.40m      outside the 1.11m envelope
progress             5.33 of the 0.80 required, still climbing at 6s
tackle               never completes
```

Rederived from the two constants that bound the geometry: `0.80` (bodies
touching) + `1.20` (`POSSESSION_CONTACT_RADIUS`) = **2.00m**, still well
inside `CHALLENGE_RANGE` (2.40m).

**The facing gate read a value nobody maintains.** `_facing_angle` is applied
to `model.rotation.y`, so the body's basis never rotates, and `_facing_angle`
itself only updates while a player turns toward somewhere they are going. A
stationary defender's facing is therefore 0 (world +Z); with the ball at −Z,
`dot = −1` and the envelope answered false on every frame. Corrected twice
before landing: first to `basis.z` (wrong — body never rotates), then to a new
`facing_direction()` accessor (real, but unmaintained when standing still).
The gate is now asked **only of a moving challenger**, because that is the
only case with a trustworthy answer; `challenge_rate`'s `closing` term already
answers "are you going at the ball" properly and continuously.

Widening the reach alone did **not** fix it — that is what proved distance was
never the blocker. With both fixes: `TACKLE COMPLETED on frame 53 at 1.35m`.

Cost: three regression assertions were red until this landed (`V0_8_4` ×2,
`V0_8_8` ×1). All three are green now.

Also capped: a stalled challenge could bank progress indefinitely (5.33 of
0.80). Now `PROGRESS_OVERFILL = 1.25`.

## 7. Animation event surface — hooks only, pack NOT integrated

| signal | fires |
|---|---|
| `ball_touched` | contact (dribble/turn/stop/pass/shot), with point, direction, strength, player velocity, foot |
| `action_started` | intent committed, one call ahead of contact, for wind-up lead time |
| `action_released` | ball gone, follow-through owns the body |
| `challenge_started` | progress crosses up from zero — the tackle animation starts before the outcome is known |
| `possession_changed` | `gained` / `lost` with a reason: `collected`, `kicked`, `dispossessed`, `ball_gone` |
| `pass_attempted` | the full pass chain (§1) |

All gated on having listeners, so they cost nothing unconnected. The
simulation emits and never reads — physics stands alone with no animation
system attached.

The `possession_changed` reason matters: a settle-or-react clip should play
when the ball is *taken*, not when the player deliberately played it.

## 8. The `RECEIVE` duty — its cost, and two fixes

`RECEIVE` is the other half of a pass: `FOLLOW_UP` gave the passer a job,
nothing ever gave the intended receiver one. It closes the defect in §2.

It also caused three regressions, all found by older suites.

### 8a. Duty churn — FIXED

Paired runs against a v0.9.0 baseline worktree, "duty changes per player per
second of settled possession", ceiling 0.30:

```
v0.9.0 baseline                     0.162  0.173  0.173  0.203
v0.9.1 as first built               0.446  0.422
v0.9.1, RECEIVE disabled            0.229  0.239  0.229  0.271
v0.9.1, player collision disabled   0.436  0.345  0.378   <- not the cause
```

Collision was the intuitive suspect and the isolation cleared it. Two fixes:
`_fill_receive` moved to *after* allocation so it overrides one player's duty
instead of erasing them from the pool and cascading a reshuffle through
everyone (twice per pass, bypassing `DUTY_RETENTION_BONUS` entirely), guarded
so it never clobbers `CONTEST` or `FOLLOW_UP` — the other two fact-based
duties. Combined with 8b, `V0_8_5` is now **ALL PASS ×3**.

`DUTY_RETENTION_BONUS` was **not** touched. It was raised on measurement noise
earlier in this milestone and reverted; the constant was never the problem.

### 8b. Attacking line collapse — FIXED

A forward is the most advanced player, so the ball is nearly always *behind*
them, and the `RECEIVE` target steered straight at it with no retreat guard —
while every other support duty has one (`LANE_MAX_RETREAT`). Every pass to a
forward dragged them back down the pitch, and a pass is in the air almost
continuously (69 receive spells over 54s, median 0.8s).

`V0_8_3` "forwards hold a clearly advanced line":

```
v0.9.0 baseline                     63%   (failed 1 of 4)
v0.9.1, RECEIVE for every role      40%, 0%, 48%, 40%
  + retreat cap vs live position    19%   (broken: the anchor moves, so it ratchets)
  + retreat cap vs formation shape  20%
  + forwards excluded from RECEIVE  68%, 78%, 68%   <- shipped
  variant: forwards receive only
  when the ball is ahead of them    54%, 62%, 59%   (worse, rejected)
```

Excluding forwards also **improved pass delivery**, the opposite of the
expected trade:

```
RECEIVE for every role      15 of 59 reached the intended man (25%)
RECEIVE excluding forwards  20 of 46 reached the intended man (43%)
```

Pulling the striker back removed the outlet and crowded the middle, so more
passes were cut out. A forward still receives passes; they are simply not
taken off their run to do it.

68% is above the v0.9.0 baseline's 63%, so the **regression is closed** — but
the assertion's bar is 80% and it still reads RED. That gap is pre-existing:
the baseline failed it too.

### 8c. Passing lanes — OPEN REGRESSION

`V0_8_7` "a useful share of in-range teammates have a clear passing lane",
bar 50%:

```
v0.9.0 baseline                     4 of 4 PASS
v0.9.1 shipped                      1 of 5 pass — 43%, 36%, 37%, 26%
  (cap only, forwards still receive)  2 of 3 pass — 62%, 53%, 48%)
```

**This is a real regression and it is not fixed.** The shipped configuration
trades it for §8a and §8b, which is net better but not free. Rendered playtest
agrees: 46% clear lane.

Cause is understood — solid bodies make players bunch, and a bunched side
offers fewer distinct lanes — but the fix is deeper positioning work, which
the brief defers until after animation.

## 9. Off-ball run separation

Two advancing players could aim at the same point. The existing crowding term
is a *weight*, and a weight gets outvoted: when the opposition leaves one open
lane, `LANE_W_CLEAR` + `LANE_W_OPEN` reward it enough for both to pick it.

Measured: 31–59 samples per run of two `PUSH_UP` players within 0.5m in the
attacking lane, of which **55–71% were also within 3m in full separation** —
genuinely the same spot, not an overlap at different depths.

Added a hard rejection (`MIN_ADVANCING_SEPARATION = 3.0`) with the fallback
ranked by *separation* rather than score — a score-ranked fallback hands both
players the same point, reproducing the exact failure.

Result: **reduced, not eliminated.** Converging samples 73/57 → 48/39/14;
genuinely-together ~30 → ~14 average, but one run still hit 37. Reported as a
partial fix. Further iteration would be fitting to variance across four
competing live-match metrics.

## 10. Final test battery — 18 suites

```
GREEN  GameplayTest, MainSceneTest, TeamSystemTest, CharacterPipelineTest,
       PersonalityTest, V0_7Match, V0_8, V0_8_1, V0_8_2, V0_8_2Oscillation,
       V0_8_8, V0_9_0, V0_9_1GameplayCleanup (45 checks)
RED    V0_8_3  forwards advanced line          — regression closed (68% vs 63% baseline), bar 80%
       V0_8_4  human monopolises possession    — PRE-EXISTING: fails 4/4 at baseline at 98%, now 95%
       V0_8_5  duty churn                      — FIXED (all pass x3)
       V0_8_6  carrier has ball before challenge — harness artifact, see below
       V0_8_7  clear passing lane              — OPEN REGRESSION (§8c)
```

`V0_8_6`'s remaining failure is deterministic and collision-caused: two
`CharacterBody3D`s created in the same frame are resolved against each other
before either has taken a physics step, so the carrier is displaced onto the
thief on frame 1. Harness construction, not gameplay.

### Test-harness corrections made (thresholds unchanged)

Each of these fixed a *measurement* that no longer described what it claimed,
with the reason recorded in the test:

- **`V0_8_6` rate limit.** A goal calls `reset_intent()`, setting
  `ai_state = -1`, which `AIController` treats as `discontinuous` and
  deliberately skips the rate limit so a stale pre-kickoff target is not
  dragged forward. Measured: **19 of 19 oversized steps within 3 frames of a
  reset, 0 unexplained.** The loop now skips 3 frames after a reset. The
  0.253m limit is untouched.
- **`V0_8_6` FOLLOW_UP** — asserted on a fixed index that had become the
  contester (`duty=0`); now picks a passer for whom the question is live.
- **`V0_8_6` human pass** — the passer now *slides around* the mate under
  solid bodies and overshoots (measured dot −1.00, the mate directly behind);
  geometry is now set up directly instead of via a timed walk.
- **`V0_8_6` / `V0_8_2`** — spawn a frame apart, and stop the loop at the
  event being measured rather than a fixed window.
- **`V0_9_1` own suite** — the poke-envelope assertion originally encoded the
  same wrong reach model as the code, so it passed *because* it shared the
  bug. Corrected to the derived geometry.

No threshold was weakened anywhere.

## 11. Rendered playtests

**AI vs AI, 45s.** Ball/carrier separation 0.92m over 994 carrier frames, 101
discrete touches (6.1/s). 43 passes, 57.3/min. **0 aimed at an opponent.**
Teammates bunched on the ball on only 6% of carrier frames. 0 frames behind a
goal line. 0 kicks by a non-carrier; kicks struck from 0.89m average, 1.59m
furthest, against a 2.4m challenge range.

Qualitatively: possession changes hands through contested challenges rather
than proximity, players hold their own space, and the ball behaves like a ball
— it runs away on a heavy touch and is recoverable.

**Human vs AI, 45s.** Human carried 69% of all carrying frames, mean ball
separation 0.62m, 58 touches. **Dispossessed 17 times, under active challenge
40% of carrying frames** — a direct improvement on the long-standing "the
human just keeps the ball" complaint. 7 of 12 human passes reached the
intended teammate (58%); launch speed delivered within 1.33 m/s of what was
asked for; 0 aimed at an opponent.

Weakest area on screen remains off-ball support: 46% clear lanes, consistent
with §8c.

## Honest summary

Closed: the critical opponent-targeting bug, the human pass aim cone, players
phasing through each other, the ball flinging players, the tackle envelope and
its facing gate, the duty-churn regression, the attacking-line collapse, and
the animation event surface.

Open and reported, not hidden:

1. **`V0_8_7` passing lanes** — a real regression from solid bodies, traded
   against two larger fixes. Bar 50%, currently 26–43%.
2. **Off-ball run convergence** — reduced by roughly half, not eliminated.
3. **`V0_8_3` forwards line** — regression closed, still under a bar the
   baseline also failed.
4. **`V0_8_6` spawn overlap** — harness artifact.

Five hypotheses were falsified by measurement during this milestone (the
facing basis, poke reach alone, the receive cascade as the churn cause,
collision as the shape cause, and the football-intuitive narrow forward
exclusion). Every one was corrected by instrumenting the exact frame rather
than reasoning from the code.
